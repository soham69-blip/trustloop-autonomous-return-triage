TRUSTLOOP SELF-LEARNING SETUP
=============================

Learning architecture:

learning/
├── __init__.py
├── feedback_store.py
├── label_mapper.py
├── model_evaluator.py
└── retraining_engine.py

Purpose:

1. Store verified human feedback.
2. Convert verified outcomes into training labels.
3. Evaluate whether new feedback is reliable.
4. Retrain only when sufficient verified data exists.
5. Never treat TrustLoop's own prediction as ground truth.
6. Never automatically train from unverified predictions.
7. Preserve the existing LightGBM training pipeline.

Important:

The existing scripts/train_lightgbm.py should remain unchanged until
the feedback pipeline has been validated.

Self-learning means:

Prediction
    ↓
Human / verified outcome
    ↓
Feedback stored
    ↓
Label validation
    ↓
Feedback quality check
    ↓
Approved training dataset
    ↓
Model evaluation
    ↓
Retraining
    ↓
New model
    ↓
Validation against previous model

The system must NOT do:

Prediction
    ↓
Assume prediction is correct
    ↓
Train on its own prediction

That would create confirmation bias and model drift.
